# Casa Cane — Project Context

## Brand
**Casa Cane** is a streetwear fashion brand selling matching clothing for dogs and humans (unisex). Based in Sydney.

## Product Line
- Starting with **hoodies and sweatshirts**
- Hero differentiator: **dog-inspired fine art prints** on the back — classical paintings (think Leonardo da Vinci, Botticelli, Michelangelo) where the human figures are replaced with cute puppies
- Products come in **human sizes (XS–3XL)** and **dog sizes (XS–XL)**
- Sold as individual pieces and as **matching sets** (human + dog bundle)

## Website Template
The site template is a single-page static HTML/CSS/JS build, inspired by SuitSupply's clean, premium aesthetic.

### Files
- `index.html` — Full page structure
- `style.css` — All styling (Playfair Display serif + Inter sans-serif)
- `script.js` — Filter, scroll effects, newsletter form

### Color Palette
- `--cream: #f5f2ed` — Background warm white
- `--sand: #e8e2d9` — Image placeholders, subtle surfaces
- `--charcoal: #1a1a1a` — Primary text and dark UI
- `--accent: #8b6f47` — Warm brown accent (buttons, badges, icons)

### Adding Product Photos
Drop images into an `assets/` folder. The HTML already references:
- `assets/hero.jpg` — Full-bleed hero image
- `assets/product-1-front.jpg` / `assets/product-1-back.jpg` — Product front/back (6 products total, numbered 1–6)
- `assets/lifestyle.jpg` — Feature band image
- `assets/print-closeup.jpg` — Print detail close-up
- `assets/look-1.jpg` through `look-4.jpg` — Lookbook grid
- `assets/about.jpg` — About section photo

All images have a "Photo coming soon" placeholder until the files exist.
