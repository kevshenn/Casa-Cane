Our Story

Born in Sydney.
Raised by dogs.

Casa Cane started as a simple question: why should your dog have boring clothes? We believe style isn't just for humans — and that the bond between a person and their dog deserves to be celebrated, loudly, on the back of a really good hoodie.

Each print is hand-commissioned and painted in the spirit of the Old Masters, then screen-printed on premium blanks we'd wear ourselves. We make small batches, always unisex, always for the whole pack.
