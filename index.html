The back is the main character

Not merch. A walking gallery with a punchline.

Casa Cane takes the drama of old master paintings and recasts it through the only lens that matters: dogs. A reaching paw instead of Adam’s hand. A long dinner table filled with very serious puppies. Cute, but not childish. Satirical, but still wearable.

The front stays restrained. The silhouette stays premium and boxy. The back carries the joke, the art, and the reason strangers stop you on the street.
